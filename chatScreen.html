Customer-Service Conversation Review

Study Purpose: This study looks at how people decide whether a customer-service conversation should be handed off to a human representative.

What You Will Do: You will read 25 customer-service conversations from an external dataset, one at a time. For each one, you will decide whether it should be transferred to a human customer-service representative, and you may optionally explain why.

Data Collected: The study records your decision for each conversation, any optional explanation you provide, an anonymous participant ID, and your IP address. The IP address is collected to help identify duplicate participation and protect the integrity of the research data. It is not displayed to other participants and is accessible only to the researcher through the study database. The conversations are not generated by you and come from an external research dataset.

Voluntary Participation: Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing the study.

How this study works

You will review 25 customer-service conversations, one at a time.

  1. Read the full conversation.
  2. Decide whether it should be transferred to a human customer-service representative.
  3. Choose Yes or No.
  4. Optionally, briefly explain your choice.
  5. Continue to the next conversation.
